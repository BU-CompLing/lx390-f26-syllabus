### What Makes Good Writing?

We begin the course by looking at writing samples and discussing what makes them good or bad. Academic papers generally have a _thesis_ that must be clearly communicated and substantiated by evidence. A well-written paper effectively communicates its thesis and its arguments supporting it, and in most cases, will give readers a comfortable reading experience.
