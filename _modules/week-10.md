### What Is Communication?

Readers' ability to understand a piece of writing is constrained by psycholinguistic processing capacity. Readers incur processing costs when faced with syntactic complexity, semantic surprisal, and violations of pragmatic maxims. Effective communicators achieve _clarity_ by distributing processing costs _uniformly_ across a text. This allows them to convey information efficiently, relieving readers the burden of _hermeneutic labor_.
